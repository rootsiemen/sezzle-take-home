Weather Lookup has been deployed.

The service is available locally through NodePort:

  http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.nodePort }}

Then try:

  curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.nodePort }}/weather?location=Minneapolis'
  curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.nodePort }}/healthz'

Response logging is enabled when the PostgreSQL auth Secret
{{ .Values.database.secretName }} is present.
