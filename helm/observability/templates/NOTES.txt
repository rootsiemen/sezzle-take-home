LGTM is available inside the cluster through the lgtm service.

Grafana is available locally through NodePort:
  http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.grafanaService.nodePort }}

Port-forward all LGTM ports locally when needed:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "lgtm.fullname" . }} 3000:3000

Then open http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.grafanaService.nodePort }} and sign in with the configured admin credentials.
The provisioned dashboard is named "Weather Lookup / RED".
